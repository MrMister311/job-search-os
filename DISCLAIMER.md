# Disclaimer

This project is a set of documents and scripts for organizing a job search. It is provided as
is, under the MIT License in `LICENSE`.

**It is not professional advice.** Nothing here is legal, financial, tax, immigration, or
career-counseling advice, and no attorney-client or advisory relationship is created by using
it. The guidance reflects one approach that worked in one search, in one field, in the United
States. Your situation may differ in ways that matter.

**Employment and compensation rules vary by jurisdiction.** Whether an employer may ask about
salary history, what a background check may cover, whether a conversation may be recorded,
what a non-compete or non-solicitation clause can enforce, and what your current employment
agreement permits are all governed by law and contract where you are, not by this repository.
Where this project touches those subjects it is describing a negotiating stance or a practical
habit, never your legal rights. Check your own jurisdiction, and consult a qualified
professional for anything consequential.

**Third-party services have their own terms.** Job boards, applicant tracking systems, and
professional networks set rules on automated access and bulk applications. Use them the way a
person would, and follow their terms of service. This project deliberately does not include
scrapers or auto-apply tools.

**Accuracy of claims about hiring.** Statistics cited in `CLAUDE.md` are sourced in the "Where
the numbers come from" section, along with an honest statement of how strong each source is.
Some are vendor research. Treat them as directional.

**Your data is yours to protect.** A populated copy of this system contains your employment
history, compensation targets, and personal contacts. Keep it private. `scripts/privacy_check.py`
checks the obvious failure modes, but you are responsible for where your copy lives and who
can see it.

**The examples are fiction.** Everything in `examples/` is invented, including the people,
employers, postings, and numbers. Any resemblance to a real organization or person is
coincidental.

**Above all, the anti-fabrication rules exist for your protection.** Do not use this system,
or any tool, to claim experience you do not have. Misrepresenting your background to an
employer can cost you the offer, the job, and more.
