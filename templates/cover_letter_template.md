Dear [Name / Hiring Team],

**Paragraph 1: Why This Company**
[Specific, genuine reason for interest in this company. Not flattery. What about their
business, mission, or situation makes this role compelling? If you cannot answer this
honestly, flag it, a generic cover letter is worse than none.]

**Paragraph 2: Why This Role**
[What about the specific scope, challenges, or responsibilities of this role matches
your trajectory and strengths. Connect directly to 1-2 items from the JD with specific
evidence from your background. Do not repeat the resume.]

**Paragraph 3: First 90 Days**
[What you would do in the first 90 days. Be specific enough to show you understand the
role but not so prescriptive that you sound like you're not listening. This paragraph
demonstrates strategic thinking.]

**Closing**
[Direct, professional close. No begging. Express interest in discussing further.]

Sincerely,
[Full Name]
