# Master Skills & Experience

This is the truth anchor for all resume content. No agent may claim experience not
documented here. When a job requires something absent from this file, it is flagged as a
gap — never filled with invention.

Items marked **[SOFT METRIC]** are directional estimates without a specific baseline; write
them as specific-without-a-number claims, never as hard data.
