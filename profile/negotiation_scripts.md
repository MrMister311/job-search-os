# Negotiation Scripts

## STATUS: TEMPLATE, personalize at intake, voice-pass with the owner, freeze before any live use. These exist BEFORE the first offer does.

Grounding: `compensation_research.md` percentiles and `target_roles.md`. Every number must
be walkable: the target range traces to researched percentiles, not to want.

## Standing rules (universal)

1. **Never disclose current compensation.** Many states bar employers from requiring it;
   volunteering it anchors low. Anchor to the researched target range and the posted band.
2. If an application already stated a range, that number is the anchor of record;
   contradicting your own application is a consistency failure. Negotiate upward via total
   comp, not by moving the stated base.
3. A recruiter's comp question in a first call is a screen, not a negotiation. Answer
   cleanly, return to fit.

## Script 1: the expectations question (first call)

> "For base I'm targeting [range] depending on total package and scope. I saw the posted
> band, so I know we're in the neighborhood, I'm more focused right now on whether the
> role is the right fit."

If pressed for current comp: "I keep current comp separate from the market rate for this
role, the range I gave you is what I'm targeting for this scope." Once, calmly. Twice:
"That's not something I share, but I'm being straight with you about my range." Move on.

## Script 2: the offer arrives

Never accept or negotiate in the moment. One move only:
> "Thank you, I'm genuinely excited. I'd like to review the full package and come back by
> [2–3 business days out]. Can you send everything in writing?"
Then reconstruct total comp, check the research, and write the counter with the full
picture on the table.

## Script 3: the counter call (structure)

1. Gratitude + enthusiasm, one beat.
2. **One ask, specific, justified by scope or market, never by need.** Then silence.
3. If base is capped, levers in order: signing bonus, equity, bonus target, accelerated
   review with a defined path, title.
4. Close warm regardless.

## Never say

- Current salary, or anything that lets it be derived
- "I need," "I was hoping," "would it be possible"
- Anything about a current-employer grievance or internal negotiation
- A number below the floor, even hypothetically
- Two asks in one counter
