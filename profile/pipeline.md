# Pipeline

Every role screened, applied to, and its status. One row per opportunity. This file is the
scoreboard, if a role is not here, it does not exist.

**Target cadence: 5–10 screened per week, 3–5 applied.** Volume with screening is the engine.

**Follow-up discipline:** every SENT application carries a **follow-up-due date** in its
Next-action cell, set at send time (default 10 days after the last outbound touch or
substantive reply). At every session start, scan Active rows for overdue clocks first.
**Maximum two silent follow-ups**; after the second, record no-response. That is the
honest move, not persistence. Follow-up notes are 60–120 words: restate interest in the
specific role, one concrete value-reminder drawn ONLY from the submitted materials (no new
claims, Rule 1 applies to follow-ups), one polite timeline question. Never "just checking
in." Unsent packages carry no follow-up clock, a decision or deadline clock instead.

**Row hygiene:** (1) **freeze the JD verbatim at screen time** (postings mutate and die);
(2) the **Human contact? cell is required:** blank must trace to a logged warm-path
attempt in `contacts.md`; (3) **stamp stage dates** in the Status cell (screened / sent /
first reply / each round / outcome) so channel conversion is computable from this file.
