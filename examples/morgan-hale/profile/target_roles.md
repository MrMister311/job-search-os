# Target Roles, Compensation Floor, and Constraints: Morgan Hale (fictional)

- **Compensation floor:** $120K base. Below this the answer is no.
- **Target range:** $135–150K base. Basis: two posted Portland/remote bands for Senior Manager, Workplace at 400–1,000-person companies ($128–160K) and a level guide putting the 50th percentile near $140K.
- **Geography:** Portland metro hybrid (up to 3 days/week) or remote. **Not relocating.** No more than occasional travel.
- **Role titles in scope:** Senior Manager / Director, Workplace Operations; Workplace Experience; Office Operations; Head of Workplace at smaller companies.
- **Out of scope, always:** facilities engineering roles; executive assistant hybrids; roles where events are the whole job.
