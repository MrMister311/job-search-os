# Senior Manager, Workplace Experience: Lumen Robotics (fictional JD, frozen 2026-08-14)

Lumen Robotics builds warehouse automation. We are ~700 people, headquartered in Portland with
a hybrid model (two days a week in office) and small offices in Austin and Raleigh.

**About the role.** You will own the Portland workplace end to end and set the standard for
our two smaller offices: space planning, vendor management, hybrid programs, events, and the
day-to-day experience of everyone who walks in the door. You will manage a team of four and
partner closely with People, IT, and Finance.

**What you will do**
- Run space planning and any future moves or expansions, including our planned 2027 Portland expansion
- Own the workplace vendor portfolio and its budget, with a focus on cost discipline
- Design and continuously improve our hybrid workplace programs, including desk booking and neighborhood seating
- Lead company events and the annual offsite (~600 attendees)
- Build and coach a workplace team of four, including our Austin and Raleigh office leads
- Define workplace metrics and report them to leadership quarterly

**What we are looking for**
- 7+ years in workplace, office, or corporate operations, with at least 2 managing people
- Experience leading an office move, consolidation, or build-out
- Hands-on experience with desk-booking or hybrid workplace tooling
- Vendor management and contract negotiation experience with measurable results
- Comfortable owning a budget and reporting on it
- Based in the Portland area or willing to be in the office two days a week

**Nice to have:** experience supporting multiple offices; exposure to IWMS platforms; IFMA
coursework.

**Compensation:** $130,000–$155,000 base, plus bonus and equity.
